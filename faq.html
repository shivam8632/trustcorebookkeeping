Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our bookkeeping services.

What services do you offer?
We provide monthly bookkeeping, bank reconciliations, financial reporting, invoicing, and expense tracking services.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes. We support startups, freelancers, and small to mid-sized businesses across different industries.
Which accounting software do you use?
We work with QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, and other cloud-based accounting platforms.
How often are financial records updated?
Your books are updated monthly to keep records accurate, organized, and tax-ready.
Can you fix messy or overdue books?
Yes. We offer bookkeeping clean-up and catch-up services for missing or disorganized records.
Will I receive financial reports each month?
Yes. Monthly reports help you understand your business performance and cash flow clearly.
Is my business information confidential?
Absolutely. All financial records and client information are handled securely and privately.
Do you help during tax season?
Yes. We prepare organized financial records and coordinate with your tax professional if needed.
How is your pricing structured?
Pricing depends on the volume of transactions and the level of bookkeeping support required.
How can I get started?
Contact us for a consultation, and we'll recommend the best bookkeeping package for your business.
